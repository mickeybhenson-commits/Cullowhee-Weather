NISAR (NORAD 65053, COSPAR 2025-163A) launched 2025-07-30 on GSLV-F16 from Sriharikota. Orbit is SGP4-propagated in your browser from a TLE at epoch —; accuracy degrades over weeks, so pass times drift the further this is opened from that date. Passes are found by sweeping forward from 10 Sep 2026 and testing whether Cullowhee lies inside the right-looking L-band swath (33–47° look angle, ~240 km). NISAR’s actual observation plan may skip individual passes; times are geometric, not a data-acquisition schedule. Orbital data: CelesTrak / Satcat · basemap: Natural Earth.
