What this is, and what it is not
A third independent opinion alongside NOAH's own engine and the National Water Model — NSSL FLASH runs the CREST hydrologic model on MRMS rainfall at 1 km / 10 min, and unit streamflow is scale-free, so every sub-basin is judged against the same published number regardless of drainage area. It is modeled, not measured: it can inform a Watch, and it never raises a Warning or an Emergency on its own — those require a measured stream rise. Forcing caveat: FLASH eats MRMS, and this watershed sits in the KGSP radar beam-blockage gap, so a FLASH miss here may be a radar miss rather than a model miss.
