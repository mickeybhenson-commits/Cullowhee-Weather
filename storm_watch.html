NOAH Storm Track Watch — Cullowhee corridor

Jackson County situational-awareness display · NHC tropical guidance

This screen watches Atlantic tropical systems and flags when a forecast track threatens the Cullowhee Creek corridor. It draws live National Hurricane Center guidance — forecast track, cone, and past track — and tests every active storm against one fixed corridor criterion.

This is a situational-awareness display, not an official flood warning. It is a display mirror: it shows the same corridor test the backend runs, but it never issues or triggers a warning, and the authoritative readiness floor is computed server-side. Use it alongside — never in place of — NWS products and your own judgment. Do not use it as the sole basis for issuing public alerts.
The corridor test

A storm counts when its forecast track crosses west of −83.2° longitude between 33.0° and 36.5°N at tropical‑storm strength or greater. How soon it gets there sets the readiness floor:

FLOOR: NONE no storm meets the corridor test
FLOOR: ELEVATED gate crossing within 72 hours
FLOOR: WATCH_PENDING gate crossing within 48 hours
Reading the screen
  • Top left — the current floor, and the reason for it.
  • Right — active Atlantic systems. Each shows name, class, and wind, with a CORRIDOR line beneath: amber meets the gate, orange meets it inside 48 h, grey does not meet it.
  • Bottom left — data status. Green means the last NHC fetch succeeded. If a fetch fails the display holds the last good render and flags it rather than going blank — check this line before trusting what you see.
  • Bottom right — legend: dashed gold is the corridor gate, blue is the NHC forecast track, dotted grey are the analog tracks.

Live guidance refreshes automatically every 15 minutes.

Two modes

Live (the view you start in) polls NHC and shows present conditions. What‑if is a practice sandbox that replays historical storms through the same corridor test:

  1. Click WHAT-IF in the header. A SIMULATION tag appears and the badge switches to SIM FLOOR.
  2. Pick a storm — Helene 2024, Frances 2004, or Ivan 2004.
  3. Press ► Play storm to advance the track hour by hour; press again to pause.
  4. Drag the scrub bar to step through manually. The T+ readout shows hours from the start of the track.

Click WHAT-IF again to leave the sandbox and return to live guidance — what‑if never affects the live floor.

The analog tracks are schematic approximations for practice — timing and intensity are not HURDAT2 data.

Needs internet

The basemap, NHC guidance, and cone all load from the web, so open it on a connected device.
